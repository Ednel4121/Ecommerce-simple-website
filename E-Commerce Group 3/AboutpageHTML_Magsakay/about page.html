About Us

Nike is a leading platform...

Nike is a global sportswear company founded in 1964, now known for its innovative athletic shoes, apparel, and equipment. The company, originally named "Blue Ribbon Sports," was renamed Nike in 1971, taking inspiration from the Greek goddess of victory. Nike's mission is to bring inspiration and innovation to every athlete in the world.
